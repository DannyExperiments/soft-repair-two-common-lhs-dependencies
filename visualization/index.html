Soft repair of four blocks

Choose which facts to retain. Removing a fact costs 640. Retained pairs pay 128 if their B blocks differ and 1 if their C labels differ.

Retained facts, by block and C label

Why the triangle is cheaper

Blocks 1, 2 and 3 come from a triangle. Block 4 is isolated and uses private padding labels p and q. Each retained pair with a shared label avoids one unit of C cost. Among three full blocks, holding the deletion and B penalties fixed, the triangle minimizes C cost by sharing all three edge labels.
